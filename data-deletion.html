Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Sales Connector gives you control over your data. This page explains how organizations, data and accounts can be deleted, and who can do it.

1. Deleting your organization or its data

Any customer can delete their organization, or delete data inside it, from the organization settings in their workspace:

  1. Sign in to your Sales Connector workspace (web or mobile app).
  2. Open Settings and then your Organization settings.
  3. Choose the data you want to remove (for example contacts, conversations or other records), or choose to delete the organization.
  4. Confirm the request.

2. Deleting the account

Only the owner of an account can delete the account and its related database. Deleting an account permanently removes the account and the data stored under it, including contacts, conversation history, pipelines, automations and settings. This cannot be undone.

3. Partner accounts

Partner accounts cannot be deleted by the partner, because a partner account is connected to several customer organizations and deleting it would affect all of them. Partners who need a change should coordinate through their account manager.

4. Need help with deletion or anything else?

If you want help with a deletion request or any other request, reach out directly to your partner or your account person. They will take care of it for you.

5. What happens to your data

  • Data you delete is removed from our active systems.
  • Some information may be kept for a limited time where required by law, for security or fraud prevention, or in routine backups until they expire.
  • Messages already delivered through WhatsApp or other connected services remain with those services, under their own policies.
  • You can disconnect connected services such as Google Calendar and WhatsApp Business at any time.
