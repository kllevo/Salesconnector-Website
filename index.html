Questions, answered

Is Sales Connector a full CRM or just a WhatsApp tool?

It is a complete CRM — contacts, pipelines, appointments, analytics — with WhatsApp messaging, chatbots, AI and automation built in.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account?

Yes, WhatsApp features run on the WhatsApp Business Platform. Our team guides you through verification and setup.

How does automation work?

Pick a trigger, add steps such as waiting, sending an AI or template reply, or moving a deal to a new stage, and it runs automatically.

Can I move my existing contacts over?

Yes. You can import contacts and leads from a spreadsheet and keep your pipeline stages.

How is my data secured?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted and monitored. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What does pricing include?

Plans scale with your team and features. Message fees charged by WhatsApp/Meta and AI usage are billed separately at cost.
