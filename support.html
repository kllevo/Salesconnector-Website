Support

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Sales Connector is provided to businesses through our partners. Support for Sales Connector is delivered by your partner.

How to get help

For any question or issue, including setup, your account, billing, features, or technical problems, please reach out directly to your partner or your account person. They know your workspace and will help you.

Common requests

  • Using the app: ask your partner or account person for guidance and training.
  • Account or billing questions: contact your partner or account person.
  • Deleting data or your account: see Account & Data Deletion, or ask your partner or account person.
  • Privacy questions: see our Privacy Policy.

Sales Connector does not provide direct customer support. Your partner is your first and main point of contact.
