Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Sales Connector is a service operated by Vips Media ("Vips Media"), which makes it available to partners under the Sales Connector name. KlevoAI is a trade name of Vips Media. This Privacy Policy explains how Sales Connector ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use our website, apps, webhooks and APIs (the "Services").

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email, company, role and a hashed password.
  • Contact data you import: your customers' and leads' names, phone numbers, emails and message history.
  • Usage data: device, browser, IP address, pages viewed and product events.
  • Payment data: handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Cookies: a small number of essential cookies for sign-in and aggregate usage.
  • Mobile app permissions: microphone (voice messages) and notifications (alerts).

2. Connected services

Google Calendar (only if you connect it): we access calendars and availability and create, update or delete appointment events. Google user data is not sent to third-party AI tools and is not used to train models.

WhatsApp Business (only if you connect it): we process messages, conversation history, templates, phone numbers and delivery/read analytics. This data is shared with Meta/WhatsApp only to deliver messages, when required to provide the Services, when required by law, or when you authorise it.

3. How we use information

To provide and secure the Services, process payments, provide support, and improve the product. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Customer data and roles

For your end-customer data, you (the workspace owner) are the controller and we act as processor. We do not sell this data or use it to train models for other customers.

5. AI processing

AI agents, chatbot replies and message drafting use a third-party AI provider (OpenAI, standard API tier). It receives only message content and configuration needed for the feature and does not train its models on API data.

6. Sub-processors

We use providers for hosting, email, error tracking, WhatsApp API access and payments. A list is available on request.

7. Webhooks and APIs

You choose the endpoints that receive your data. Data sent there is governed by the endpoint owner's practices.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Production access is restricted, audited and monitored, and we run a responsible-disclosure programme.

9. Retention

We keep data while your account is active and as needed to provide the Services or meet legal obligations. On account closure, data is deleted or returned to you on request.

10. Your rights

You may access, correct, delete or export your personal data, object to or restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. See our Account & Data Deletion page for how to delete your data or account.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, data access or deletion requests, email contactus@klevoai.com. We aim to respond within 3 to 5 days.
